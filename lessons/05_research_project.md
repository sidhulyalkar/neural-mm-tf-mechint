# Lesson 5 — Build a result you would trust from someone else

Choose one phenomenon: sycophancy, refusal, factual correction, multilingual transfer, self-correction, uncertainty, or another tightly defined behavior.

Your final project must include:

- preregistered metric and clean/corrupted pair construction;
- at least one observational localization method;
- at least one exact causal intervention;
- a negative control;
- held-out semantic examples;
- a collateral-damage metric;
- a necessity or sufficiency test;
- a failure case;
- a claim written at the strength justified by the evidence.

The highest-value outcome may be discovering that your original mechanistic story was wrong.
