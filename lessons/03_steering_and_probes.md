# Lesson 3 — Representation directions that earn causal status

A mean-difference direction is one of the simplest representation hypotheses. It becomes interesting only after intervention.

## Exercise

1. collect neutral and pressured final-token residual states;
2. estimate a pressure direction on a training split;
3. measure held-out probe performance;
4. steer with coefficients from negative to positive;
5. plot target logit difference and output KL;
6. compare against ten equal-norm random directions.

A real mechanistic signal should create a structured dose-response with better specificity than random directions.
